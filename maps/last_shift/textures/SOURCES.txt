manifest.json records exact source URLs, dimensions, file sizes, provider MD5
hashes and locally verified SHA-256 hashes for every downloaded image. Tile
dimensions are in metres, converted from the official API's millimetre values.

Diffuse images are 2048 pixels square. Normal GL, Normal DX and roughness
images are 1024 pixels square. GL and DX normals are alternative conventions;
choose the appropriate one for the renderer rather than binding both at once.
