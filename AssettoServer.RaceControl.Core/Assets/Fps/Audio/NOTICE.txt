ASRC FPS audio asset notice

Recorded footsteps (six files)

footstep_boot_01.wav through footstep_boot_06.wav are adapted from "Footsteps"
by GboxMikeFozzy, published on OpenGameArt on 14 April 2022 under CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/footsteps-0
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Originals: 01-footstep_0.ogg and 02-footstep.ogg through 06-footstep.ogg.
Changes: mono 44.1 kHz PCM16 conversion, gentle high/low-pass filtering and EQ,
4 ms onset/18 ms release fades, level matching with a -6 dBFS peak ceiling.
No synthetic layers, pitch shifts, or additional reverb were added.
The original source URLs, hashes, processing and import date are in audio-manifest.json.

Recorded assault rifle (one file)

fire_assault_rifle_02.wav is adapted from the close AR-15/M4 recording D_32P.wav
in The Free Firearm Sound Library, created and recorded by Ben Jaszczak,
Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras and Matthew Nanney; published under CC0 1.0.
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Changes: isolated the first shot at 0.701 seconds into a 0.5-second mono 44.1 kHz
PCM16 clip; 55 Hz high-pass, 9 kHz low-pass, 0.5 ms onset/100 ms release fades,
and -1 dBFS peak normalization. No synthetic layers, pitch shifts or added reverb.
Original source hash, trim offsets, processing and import date are in audio-manifest.json.

Generated effects (the other 47 WAV files)

The other WAV files were generated for this project with the ElevenLabs
Sound Effects API under the paid plan authorized by the project owner. The
generation used the eleven_text_to_sound_v2 model and did not use a cloned or
third-party voice. The generated outputs are redistributed as part of the ASRC FPS
client pack under the commercial-use rights applicable to that paid plan.

fire_desert_eagle_03.wav intentionally reuses fire_desert_eagle_01.wav. Its manifest
retains the original generation prompt/date and records copyOf; the rejected third
variant is not distributed. The 54 named catalog entries therefore contain 53 unique sounds.

Generation prompts, normalized-file hashes, durations, and technical format details
are recorded in audio-manifest.json. The API credential is not stored in this
repository or in the generated client pack.
