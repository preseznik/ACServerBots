AssettoServer Race Control modifications: model parts were regrouped by material,
reoriented around the grip, textures were downsampled for real-time use, and the
FBX source was converted to Assetto Corsa KN5. The first-person variant includes
project-owned placeholder arms; the weapon geometry and textures remain ELIZION's work.
